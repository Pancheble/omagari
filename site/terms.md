# Terms of use

[한국어](/ko/terms)

These terms are for the **hosted Omagari server** at `app.omagari.org`, run by Chan Baek ("we"). If your team runs its own server, these terms don't apply: your team is its own operator. Our [privacy policy](/privacy) says what we do with your data.

Last updated: 2026-11-21

## 1. What this is

The hosted server is a free **beta**. It can change, stop, or lose data, and we may turn people away. We try to keep it up and back it up, but we don't promise it. The [status page](https://status.omagari.org) shows when it's down.

## 2. Accounts and access

- You sign in with GitHub. To get a workspace, you request access for a GitHub account (an organization or your own), and the operator approves or denies it.
- By requesting, you say you may act for that account, and you agree to these terms and the [privacy policy](/privacy).
- Install the Omagari GitHub App only on repos you're allowed to share with us. When you share a repo, you choose a share level: `full` sends the content of your changed files to this server, and `declared` sends paths only.

## 3. What you can't do

- Don't use the server to break the law, or to get into repos, accounts, or data that aren't yours.
- Don't put in other people's secrets or personal data that you have no right to share.
- Don't try to get around the limits, probe the server for weak spots, or put a heavy load on it. To report a vulnerability, see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/omagari-dev/omagari/blob/dev/SECURITY.md).

## 4. Your content

What you put in is yours: branch names, changed files, status lines, chat, Q&A, and debate patches. You let us store and handle it only as needed to run the service for you and your workspace, as the privacy policy describes. We don't use it to train models and we don't sell it.

## 5. Deleting and ending

- You can delete your account, a repo's data, or a workspace yourself, as described in the [privacy policy](/privacy#delete).
- We can limit or end access if someone breaks these terms or puts the server at risk. We'll tell you why when we can.
- If we shut the hosted beta down, we'll say so on the status page ahead of time when we can. Omagari doesn't have an export yet, and your code lives in your Git repos, so nothing there depends on us.

## 6. No warranty, limited liability

The beta comes "as is". We don't promise it works without errors or is always available. As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for lost data or indirect losses from using it. This doesn't limit liability that the law doesn't allow us to limit.

## 7. Changes

We may change these terms. We'll put the new version here with a new date and, for big changes, say so in the web app. If you keep using the server after that, the new terms apply; if you don't agree, delete your account.
